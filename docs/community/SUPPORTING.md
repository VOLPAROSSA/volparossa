# Support VOLPAROSSA

VOLPAROSSA is an early-stage open-source effort to make connectivity, storage and
useful computation cooperative infrastructure. Support can fund careful engineering,
independent verification and accessible documentation. There is no supported
end-user release, claimed revenue or established commercial traction here.

This page publishes no live sponsorship or payment offer. A payment link may be
published only after its destination is verified as usable; a dated application
or support request is not proof of current availability. No funding is required
to participate in development or the network.

You can help now through a [bounded contribution](../../CONTRIBUTING.md), an
independent reproduction, accessibility feedback or relevant research. General
ideas belong in [central Discussions](https://github.com/VOLPAROSSA/volparossa/discussions).
For a private research or support inquiry, the organization publishes
**contact@volparossa.org**. A conversation does not create a partnership or commitment.

## Work that support could advance

These are engineering priorities, not funded promises or delivery dates. See the
[current plan](../IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md#execution-priorities-and-next-milestones)
for exact source revisions, dependencies and original failed trials.

Useful first contributions include one safe reproduction or a scoped documentation
fix. Accessibility feedback should include independent screen-reader/device checks
where possible. Gather voluntary aggregate feedback, without adding analytics or
collecting private usage data; choose a reporting cadence maintainers can sustain.

| Priority | A meaningful outcome | Resources to establish before committing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared private storage | Image/Cloud publication, maintenance through provider loss, repeated restoration, deletion and confirmed zero provider usage. | Maintainer and reviewer time; bounded disposable network trials; storage for public acceptance artifacts. |
| Protected remote compute | Verified guest runtime and public model prerequisites, then admissible confidential hardware and private-task acceptance without weaker fallback. | Reproducible build/test time; an explicitly suitable confidential-computing environment; independent security review. Ordinary hosted VMs are not proof of confidentiality. |
| Contributor access and reproducibility | A newcomer can follow one safe path and explain the result and remaining limits. | Documentation/design/review time and participants willing to report actual onboarding barriers. |

Estimate a bounded milestone from recorded trial duration and reproducible resource
requirements before setting a budget. Publish the scope, ceiling, evidence required
for success and what happens if it fails. An award ceiling or hardware quotation
is not a project cost estimate. Do not promise a release date before the relevant
acceptance evidence exists.

## Different kinds of support

| Mechanism | What it means here |
| --- | --- |
| Donation or recurring sponsorship | Voluntary support without ownership, privileged network access or guaranteed delivery. Any recognition should be optional. |
| In-kind help | Specific reviewed equipment, test capacity, accessibility work or expert time; suitability, access and handling of private data must be agreed first. |
| Research grant | A restricted award with applicant, geography, eligible-cost, disclosure and reporting conditions. Work already started may be ineligible. |
| Research collaboration | An independently agreed question and evaluation with an appropriate academic or nonprofit partner. No current partnership is implied. |
| Commercial agreement | Separately scoped development or support obligations with appropriate legal and tax arrangements. Not a disguised donation or a promise of production readiness. |
| Equity investment | Ownership, due diligence and company governance; a possible later route, not an offer of shares or a current fundraising round. |

No registered company, charity status, tax deductibility or ability to accept a
particular payment is asserted. No agreement is formed by this page. Real-money
services and the experimental Bank/Transaction-layer are separate matters.

## Transparency and independence

Funding must not purchase access to private user information, policy votes, release
signatures, review exemptions or preferential overlay participation. A sponsor
cannot override privacy or turn unverified functionality into a release claim.

When funding becomes available, publish a modest record of purpose, funds received
and spent in appropriate aggregates, funded scope, evidence achieved, blockers and
remaining balance. Keep donor identities private unless they consent to recognition;
never publish banking, tax or private account records. Reporting cadence must fit
actual maintainer capacity; there is no paid support or response-time promise.

The project uses AI-assisted development. Funding and upstream rules must be checked
before an application or funded task. In particular, [NLnet's policy dated 20 October
2026](https://nlnet.nl/foundation/policies/generativeAI/2.0) restricts both applications
and funded artifact production. The present engineering workflow cannot simply be
represented as compliant. Factual opportunity research is not application text.

For evidence of present capabilities and limitations, start with the
[technical overview](OVERVIEW.md) and the [current evidence index](../IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md).
